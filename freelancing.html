How I work

Four steps, no surprises

  1. 01

    Understand & challenge

    We start with your problem. I ask questions, challenge assumptions and pin down what success looks like, not just what the spec says.

  2. 02

    Architecture & plan

    A clear architecture saves projects: maintainable, testable, with documented interfaces and a realistic path to certification.

  3. 03

    Build alongside

    Regular check-ins, reviews of designs and code, hands-on where it helps. You always know where we stand and what's next.

  4. 04

    Hand over

    Your team should own what we build. Documentation, knowledge transfer, and I'm still around for questions afterwards.
