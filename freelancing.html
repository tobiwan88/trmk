Advisory Services

In addition to my main role (CV) I offer advisory services to help you navigate the full device lifecycle — from concept through production and beyond. Whether you're building your first connected product or scaling an existing line, I help you ask the right questions before you're locked into expensive decisions.

My core expertise lies in the intersection of hardware constraints, firmware architecture, and radio protocols — for devices that must survive in the real world without constant attention. I can support you in:

  • Short range radio protocols: Zigbee, Thread, BLE or EnOcean
  • Long range: LoRa
  • Cellular: LTE-M / NB-IoT
  • Regulatory landscape (CE, FCC, and many more rules depending on your market)

Setting up data pipelines and on-device processing using Tiny-ML technologies.
